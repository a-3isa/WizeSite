Approach B — at a glance

• Two dropdowns in the top bar: workspace (small) + project (prominent, orange).

• Everything below is scoped to the selected project — net, funds-in, expenses, recent list.

• Adds a project-only budget-used bar (spent vs contract value) you can't show at workspace level.

• Payment = funds in (green +). Expense = funds out (red −). Clear money direction.

• Workspace Quick access kept for navigation, per your call.

• Difference vs Approach A: A is a tighter workspace dashboard; B makes the dashboard a project cockpit with a project selector.

Today Add-Payment has no category at all. Proposed: a distinct "Payment type" list (payment-flavored), so expense categories and payment types no longer collide. Confirm this list and I'll wire it.

Differentiation summary

Expense → Category = what the money was spent on (materials, equipment…).

Payment → Payment type = who/why you paid (supplier, worker wage, contractor…).

Separate label sets, separate constants, no shared dropdown — so changing one never disturbs the other.

Commas now appear live in every number field app-wide: amount, wage, days, quantity, rate, contract value, budget, paid amount.
