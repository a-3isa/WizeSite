The idea

Construction runs on crumpled receipts, a notebook in the pickup, and forty WhatsApp voice notes a day. Buildozer turns all of that into one clear number: are we making money on this job?

Every contractor knows the feeling. The building is going up, money is going out, and nobody can say — today, right now — whether the project is still profitable. The truth lives in a site engineer's pocket, a foreman's memory and an accountant's spreadsheet that's two weeks behind.

Buildozer was born on that gap. It's not an ERP you need a training course for. It's the tool you open between pouring a slab and paying a truck driver — with dusty hands, one bar of signal, in whatever language you think in.

Say what you paid. It understands. Tap confirm. Your office sees it, your profit updates, and you get back to building.

Before
